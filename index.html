The Money Archive

Money, power and the history behind them

The Money Archive is an independent educational YouTube channel. We publish short documentaries (8 to 10 minutes) about economic history: financial crises and bubbles, central banks and money, trade and geopolitics, the technology companies that changed the world, and the people and empires behind them.

Every video is researched with cited sources, fact-checked, and produced as original motion graphics using licensed public-domain archive images. Captions are available in eleven languages.

Watch on YouTube: @MoneyArchiveDocs

The Money Archive Uploader

To publish consistently, the channel uses a private, internal tool that uploads our own finished videos to our own channel through the YouTube Data API, together with their thumbnails, captions and translated titles and descriptions. It is used only by the channel owner, is not offered to other users, and does not access anyone else's data.
